## 🛠️ Mechanical Design (SolidWorks)

### CAD Design Highlights
- Designed entirely in **SolidWorks**
- Segmented cylindrical body architecture
- Each segment designed to:
  - Expand radially for anchoring
  - Contract axially for thrust
- Geometry supports **future soft-material casting**

### Design Artifacts
- **Complete CAD models**
- **Section views and exploded concepts**
- **Locomotion sequence diagrams**

## ⚙️ Actuation Concept

This project focuses on **mechanical feasibility**, with actuation proposed conceptually:

| Actuation Method | Suitability |
|------------------|-------------|
| Pneumatic | Strong deformation, common in soft robots |
| SMA (Shape Memory Alloy) | Compact, suitable for micro-scale |
| Tendon-Driven | Centralized actuation, scalable |

Final actuation choice is left open for future prototyping.

## 🧪 Materials & Manufacturing (Proposed)

### Materials
- Silicone elastomers (Ecoflex / Dragon Skin)
- TPU outer skin for durability
- Fiber reinforcement for directional strain control

### Manufacturing Approach
1. Mold-based silicone casting
2. Segment-wise chamber fabrication
3. Reinforcement embedding
4. Assembly into modular chain

## ⚠️ Challenges & Design Considerations

### 1. Anchoring Reliability
- Risk of slip if friction is symmetric  
- Solution: anisotropic surface textures or micro-features

### 2. Actuation at Micro-Scale
- Pneumatics become complex at small scale  
- SMA or tendon-based actuation recommended

### 3. Durability
- Soft materials prone to wear  
- Replaceable outer skins suggested
